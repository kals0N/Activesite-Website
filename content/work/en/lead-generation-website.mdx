## The challenge

Many service businesses already get visitors from Google, social media and word of mouth, but very few of those visitors get in touch. The usual causes are a vague headline, a slow site on mobile data and contact details buried at the bottom of the page.

## Strategy and solution

We start from the number of new clients the business needs each month. Then we work backwards to the pages, messages and actions that will produce them.

- **One clear path.** Every page leads to a single action: book a free consultation or send a WhatsApp message.
- **Proof beside every call to action.** Results, reviews and guarantees sit next to the buttons, where people decide.
- **Built for phones.** A fast, mobile-first build so visitors on mobile data do not give up.
- **Measured from day one.** Every form, call and WhatsApp click is tracked, so we know which pages bring clients.
