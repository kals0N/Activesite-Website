## The challenge

[[PLACEHOLDER: describe the client's situation before the project in two or three sentences.]] Visitors arrived from search and social media, but very few contacted the business. The old site was slow on mobile and buried the contact details.

## Strategy and solution

We started from the number of new clients the business needed each month. Then we worked backwards to the pages, messages and actions that would produce them.

- **One clear path.** Every page leads to a single action: book a free consultation.
- **Proof beside every call to action.** Results, reviews and guarantees sit next to the buttons.
- **Built for phones.** A fast, mobile-first build so visitors on mobile data do not give up.
- **Measured from day one.** Every form, call and WhatsApp click is tracked.
