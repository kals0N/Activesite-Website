## The challenge

When the logo, social posts and website all look different, prospects cannot see the quality of the work. They compare on price, because nothing signals a premium service.

## Strategy and solution

We define who the brand serves and what it should feel like in one sentence. Every design decision is tested against that sentence.

- **A distinctive mark** that works at favicon size and on a billboard.
- **A colour and type system** that keeps every post and document consistent.
- **A portfolio website** that presents the best work as outcomes, not just images.
- **Templates** so the client can publish on-brand content without a designer.
