## The challenge

[[PLACEHOLDER: describe the client's situation before the rebrand.]] The work was excellent, but the logo, social posts and website all looked different. Prospects compared on price because nothing signalled premium quality.

## Strategy and solution

We defined who the brand serves and what it should feel like in one sentence. Every design decision was tested against that sentence.

- **A distinctive mark** that works at favicon size and on a billboard.
- **A colour and type system** that keeps every post and document consistent.
- **A portfolio website** that presents the best work as outcomes, not just images.
- **Templates** so the client can publish on-brand content without a designer.
