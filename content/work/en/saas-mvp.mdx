## The challenge

[[PLACEHOLDER: describe the founder's idea and constraints.]] The founder needed a working product to show investors and early customers, without spending the whole budget on features nobody had asked for.

## Strategy and solution

We cut the idea down to the one job customers would pay for, and built that first.

- **Prototype first.** A clickable prototype tested with real users before any code.
- **A focused MVP.** Secure accounts, the core workflow and subscription billing. Nothing else.
- **Launch support.** Hosting, monitoring and analytics in place on day one.
- **Weekly iterations** based on what users actually did.
