Your clients no longer only type keywords into Google. They ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews for a recommendation, and get one answer. This guide explains how to become that answer.

## How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

To be recommended by ChatGPT and other AI assistants, make your business easy to understand and easy to verify. Describe what you do clearly and consistently everywhere, answer your clients' real questions on your website, earn mentions on trusted sites and keep your business details identical across the web.

## What are AEO and GEO?

**AEO**, answer engine optimisation, helps your content appear in direct answers, such as Google's AI Overviews and featured snippets. **GEO**, generative engine optimisation, helps AI chat tools cite or recommend your business. Both build on good SEO; they do not replace it.

## 1. Answer real questions, directly

AI tools look for clear answers. Turn your clients' questions into headings on your pages, then answer each one in the first two or three sentences. Add detail after the direct answer, not before it.

## 2. Make your business a clear "entity"

AI systems need to be sure who you are. Use the same business name, description, location and contact details on your website, Google Business Profile, social profiles and directories. Add structured data to your site so machines can read these facts.

## 3. Earn mentions on trusted sites

AI assistants often rely on sources beyond your own website: directories, reviews, press articles and community discussions. Ask happy clients for Google reviews and get listed on reputable directories in your industry.

## 4. Publish something worth citing

Original information gets cited. Share your own data, clear comparisons and practical guides that answer a question better than anyone else. A short, well-structured page beats a long, vague one.

## 5. Let AI crawlers read your site

Check that your robots.txt does not block AI crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot if you want to be cited. Some sites also publish an llms.txt file summarising who they are and their key pages.

## How long does it take?

Results vary by industry and competition. Clear answers and consistent business details can be improved in weeks. Mentions and authority build over months, so start early.
