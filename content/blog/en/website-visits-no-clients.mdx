You can see visitors in your statistics, but the phone stays quiet. This is one of the most common problems we fix. The good news: it usually comes down to a few clear causes.

## Why does my website get traffic but no leads?

Most websites with traffic but no leads have one of five problems: the message is unclear, the site is slow on phones, there is no obvious next step, there is no proof, or the form asks too much. Fix the first one you find and measure again before changing anything else.

## 1. Visitors do not understand what you offer in five seconds

Read your homepage headline aloud. Does it say what you do, for whom and what result they get? "Welcome to our website" does none of these. Replace it with a promise, such as "Accounting for small businesses in Douala, done on time every month."

## 2. Your website is slow on mobile

Most of your visitors are on a phone, often on mobile data. If a page takes more than three seconds to load, many leave before they see anything. Compress images, remove heavy plugins and test your site with Google PageSpeed Insights.

## 3. There is no clear next step

Every page should lead to one main action, such as booking a call or sending a WhatsApp message. Put that button at the top of the page and again at the end. Use action words like "Book my free call", not "Submit".

## 4. There is no proof

People do not trust claims; they trust evidence. Add short testimonials, client logos and real results next to your buttons. One specific number beats a paragraph of adjectives.

## 5. Your form asks for too much

Every extra field loses people. Ask only what you need to reply: name, email or WhatsApp, and a short message. You can ask the rest on the call.

## How do I know which problem I have?

Install analytics and watch three numbers: how many people visit, how many click your main button and how many complete the form. The biggest drop shows where to start. Session recording tools can also show where visitors hesitate.

## What should I fix first?

Fix the message first, then speed, then the call to action. These three changes often make the biggest difference for the least work. Then add proof and shorten your forms.
