Most business owners ask about price first. It is the right question, but the answer depends on what the website must do for you. This guide explains what drives the cost, so you can compare quotes fairly.

## How much does a website cost in Cameroon?

The cost of a website in Cameroon depends on three things: the type of site, the features it needs and who writes the content. A simple brochure site costs far less than an online store or a custom web app. The cheapest quote is rarely the cheapest site once you count lost clients and rebuilds.

## What type of website do you need?

Start with the job the website must do. Each type carries a different level of work.

| Type of site | Main job | Typical scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brochure or business website | Build trust and bring enquiries | 5–10 pages, contact and WhatsApp |
| Portfolio | Show your work and win better clients | Project pages, booking, newsletter |
| Online store | Sell products day and night | Catalogue, checkout, payments, shipping |
| Sales funnel | Turn traffic into leads and sales | Landing pages, email follow-up, CRM |
| Web app or SaaS | Deliver a service through software | Accounts, dashboards, billing |

## Which features change the price the most?

A few features account for most of the difference between quotes:

- **Custom design** versus a template that thousands of other businesses use.
- **Copywriting.** Words that sell take time. Many quotes leave them to you.
- **Payments**, including Mobile Money and cards.
- **Integrations** such as booking tools, CRMs and email marketing.
- **Languages.** A real English and French site is two sites' worth of content.
- **Speed and SEO work**, which decide whether anyone finds the site.

## What costs do most quotes leave out?

Ask every agency about these before you sign:

1. **Hosting and domain** renewals every year.
2. **Maintenance**: security updates, backups and fixes.
3. **Content updates** after launch.
4. **Tracking and analytics**, so you know which pages bring clients.
5. **Ownership.** Make sure you own the domain, code and content.

## Is a cheap website worth it?

A cheap website is worth it only if it brings clients. A site that looks fine but converts nobody costs you every month: in hosting, in ads that lead nowhere and in clients who choose a competitor. Judge a quote by the leads and sales it is designed to produce, not by the number of pages.

## How do you get an accurate quote?

Prepare three things before you contact an agency: your goal (for example, ten new enquiries a month), examples of sites you like and the features you are sure you need. A good agency will turn that into a written scope and timeline.
