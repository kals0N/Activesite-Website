## Legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Consent** for analytics and advertising cookies and for the newsletter. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Steps before a contract** or **performance of a contract** when you ask for a proposal, book a call or become a client.
- **Legitimate interests** for site security, spam prevention and replying to messages, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation** for accounting and tax records.

We apply equivalent principles under Cameroon's personal data protection law and, for Nigerian visitors, the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. [[PLACEHOLDER: lawyer to confirm the exact Cameroonian law reference and any registration duties]]

Some processors store data outside Cameroon, the EU or the UK, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses provided by those processors.

- Client records: for the length of the contract and as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
- Analytics: up to **14 months**.
- Cookie choice: up to **6 months**, after which we ask again.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. Email [contact@activesite.cc](mailto:contact@activesite.cc). We reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France, the ICO in the UK or the NDPC in Nigeria.

## Security

We use HTTPS, access controls and reputable providers. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive data such as passwords through our forms.
