Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We use them only for the purposes below. Analytics and marketing cookies are **off until you accept them**. You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link in the footer.

## Necessary cookies

Always on. They make the site work.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `as_consent` | Activesite | Remembers your cookie choice | 6 months |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Activesite | Remembers your language | 12 months |
| Turnstile cookies | Cloudflare | Spam protection on forms, when used | Session |

## Analytics cookies

Only with your consent.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 | Measures visits and page performance | Up to 14 months |

## Marketing cookies

Only with your consent.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_fbp` | Meta | Measures ads and builds audiences | 3 months |
| `_ttp`, `ttcsid*` | TikTok | Measures ads and builds audiences | Up to 13 months |

## Embedded services

When you open the booking calendar, Cal.com may set its own cookies to run the booking. When you click a WhatsApp link, WhatsApp's own policies apply.

## Google Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode v2. All Google storage is denied by default and only granted for the categories you accept.

## How to change your choice

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer, or delete cookies in your browser settings. Questions: [contact@activesite.cc](mailto:contact@activesite.cc).
